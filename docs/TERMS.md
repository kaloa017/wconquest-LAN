## Accounts and conduct
Choose an appropriate username, protect your password and recovery code, and do
not access another person's account without permission. Do not harass, threaten,
discriminate, share other people's private information or post illegal content.
Do not exploit bugs, automate farming, bypass inactivity checks, impersonate staff,
or deliberately overload the server. Report problems to the host.

## Gameplay
All game money, resources, markets, loans and casino outcomes are fictional. They
are not investments, bank deposits or items redeemable for real money.
War, combat and loss of territory are part of play. A recently logged-in member
of a target faction must be online when a new war is declared; after 24 hours
without logging in, that member's offline status no longer prevents declaration.
Protection does not cancel existing wars or prevent attacks during those wars.
After 30 minutes without interaction, game actions pause until you press Keep
playing. Earth and space resource production continues for at most two hours
after your last activity. Time beyond that allowance does not earn resources.

## Moderation and changes
Moderators can mute chat or temporarily time out players. Senior moderators can
also resolve territory disputes. Admins can ban or delete accounts, revoke
sessions, change staff roles, correct progression and manage game data.
Word filters may reject messages; do not try to bypass moderation filters.
Account deletion releases land, removes buildings and progression, ends related
battles, cancels associated trades/loans without further repayment and may
dissolve a faction if no members remain. Country/faction shares that are
delisted are cashed out at their last quote less the normal trading fee.
Deletion cannot be undone in the game. Ask the host about disputes or privacy
requests before requesting deletion. Older backups and free-text records require
separate review, as explained in the Privacy policy.
Actions may be reviewed by the host. Ask the host if you believe a decision was
wrong. Balance, features and these rules may change; updates are described in
the changelog. Existing progress may be lost through combat, technical faults or
an explicit host decision. Availability and preservation of progress are not guaranteed.
The host may close registrations or pause player changes for maintenance.
Production and scheduled game processing may continue during that pause.
School power, network policies and maintenance may affect availability.
